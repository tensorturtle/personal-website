I studied neuroscience, which is the discipline of inferring how a system works
when you cannot look inside it directly. Language models raise recognisably similar
questions — what a representation encodes, which parts of a system account for a
behaviour — without that constraint, since every weight and activation can be read
and altered. Anthropic's work on the biology of large models is what convinced me
the questions are real ones rather than a loose analogy.

I would like to work on this. I have the applied side of it and not the research
side, so what draws me is nearer to wanting to learn it than to being ready for it.
